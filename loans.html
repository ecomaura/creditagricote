Questions we hear often

Still unsure? Call us and we will talk it through.

What decides my interest rate?

Your credit score, income, job or business stability, the loan amount and tenure, and the lender's own policy all play a part. We help you see which lenders are likely to suit your profile.

How long does approval take?

It depends on the lender and how complete your documents are. We give you a realistic timeline when we speak and keep you updated.

Will applying hurt my credit score?

Lenders usually check your credit report when you formally apply, and many applications in a short time can lower your score. That is why we shortlist before applying.

Which documents will I need?

Usually ID proof, address proof, income proof and recent bank statements. Secured loans also need papers for the property, vehicle or gold. Each loan above lists what is typical.

Are there fees I should know about?

Lenders may charge processing fees and other charges. We explain every fee before you commit to anything.

Common questions

How do I get started?

Call us or send the form above. We will arrange a short conversation to understand what you need before suggesting anything.

Which documents might I need for a loan?

This depends on the loan type and lender. Typically it includes identity and address proof, income documents and bank statements. We will give you an exact list once we know your case.

Can I start investing with a small amount?

Many mutual funds accept small monthly SIPs. We will help you choose an amount that fits your budget and goal.

Are mutual fund returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks and returns are not assured. We help you pick funds that match your risk comfort and time horizon.

Do you only help with one service at a time?

No. Many clients begin with one need and later add others. We keep your full picture in view.

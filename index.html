Keep these documents handy

Having these ready makes your application faster. We will confirm the exact list for your loan when we speak.

Documents by loan type

Identity and address

Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter ID, and a recent utility bill or rental agreement.

Income proof

Recent salary slips or Form 16 if salaried. ITR and financials if self-employed.

Bank statements

Usually the last 6 to 12 months of your primary account.

Purpose or security papers

Property papers, vehicle quotation, admission letter or gold items, depending on the loan.
